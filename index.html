The Best Movie I've Seen Lately

The best movie I've seen lately is Project Hail Mary, based of the book by Andy Weir. The movie follows a school teacher, Dr. Grace and an alien named Rocky. Both have been sent out by their home planets on an impossible mission to save the universe, after scientists had found out many of the surrounding stars and planets were dying due to unknown causes.

Ryan Gosling takes the role of Dr. Grace, while Rocky is voiced by James Oritz. I really enjoyed the movie, it had a lot of great scenes with humour, drama, and some parts also had me at the edge of my seat. Overall, I was really taken away by all the cinematography in the film. Below, is one example I really like from the movie. I think Ryan did really well in his role, and his character had really good chemistry with the other characters in the film.

I also really enjoyed the film score from the movie. The person behind the film's music was Daniel Pemberton. And it did also feature Harry Styles' Sign of the Times. After watching, I did go back to listening to some of the tracks from the film. My personal favourite is Time Go Fishing.

If I could, I would definitely want to experience it in theatres again.
