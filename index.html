The Best Movie I've Seen Lately

The best movie I've seen lately is Project Hail Mary, based of the book by Andy Wier. The movie follows a school teacher, Dr. Grace and an alien named Rocky. Both have been sent out by their home planets on an impossible mission to save the universe, after scientists had found out many of the surrounding stars and planets were dying due to unknown causes.

Ryan Gosling takes the role of Dr. Grace, while Rocky is voiced by James Oritz. I really enjoyed the movie, it had a lot of great scenes with humour, drama, and some parts also had me at the edge of my seat. Overall, I was really taken away by all the cinematography in the film.
